# Organic Chemistry Notes

Key concepts:

- Inductive effect changes electron density through sigma bonds.
- Resonance stabilizes intermediates by delocalizing pi electrons.
- Markovnikov addition places hydrogen on the carbon with more hydrogens in many HX addition reactions.
- Steric hindrance and solvent polarity can change mechanism preference.

JEE concept:

For mechanism questions, identify the electrophile, nucleophile, leaving group, and most stable intermediate before choosing the product.

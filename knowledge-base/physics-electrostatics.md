# Electrostatics Notes

Key formulas:

- Coulomb force: `F = k q1 q2 / r^2`
- Electric field: `E = F / q`
- Electric potential due to point charge: `V = kq / r`
- Electric flux through a closed surface: `Phi = q_enclosed / epsilon_0`

JEE concept:

For a point charge at the center of a cube, total flux through the cube is `q / epsilon_0`.
By symmetry, flux is equally divided among six faces, so flux through one face is `q / (6 epsilon_0)`.
